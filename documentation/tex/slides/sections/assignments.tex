\subsection{The assignments}

\begin{frame}
  \frametitle{The assignments lower the pass mark}
Two assignments are offered, one for each chapter, each marked out of 25.
They are optional: submit one, both or neither.

\pause

Their marks are not added to the questionnaire.
They lower its pass mark instead:
score $a_1$ on the first and $a_2$ on the second,
and you pass with a questionnaire score of at least
\[
60 - a_1 - a_2 ,
\]
which is 10 out of 100 for full marks on both.

\pause

An assignment is submitted as a pull request into the course repository:
a branch, the tests run, the pull request opened,
and the submission complete once continuous integration has passed on it.
\end{frame}
