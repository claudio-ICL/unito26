\subsection{What we learn about quantitative finance}

\begin{frame}
  \frametitle{What we build in each chapter}
In Chapter 1 we fold the stream of orders into an aggregated order book,
examine several implementations of that fold,
and compare their performance.

\pause

We then implement Hawkes processes as a statistical model for the arrival of orders,
and study what the parametrisation of such a process says about a trading epoch.

\pause

We arrive at the order flow imbalance and price formation,
which is the end point of the chapter.

\pause

In Chapter 2 we write pricers for European calls and puts,
under Black--Scholes and then under SABR,
and quantify the profit and loss of strategies built to capture a volatility signal.
\end{frame}
