\subsection{Examination and assignment}

\begin{frame}
  \frametitle{The assignment lowers the pass mark}
\emph{Questionnaire}: a snippet of code, and what it computes. Out of 100.\\
\emph{Assignment}: one, optional. Out of 50.

\[
\text{pass mark} \; = \; 60 - a .
\]

Submitted as a pull request. Sample questions published during the course.
\note{
The examination is a multiple-choice questionnaire. A question shows a snippet of code and
asks what it computes, or shows several and asks which is the right one for a stated
purpose. It is marked out of 100, and the pass mark is 60.
There is one assignment, the order flow imbalance on recorded data, and it is optional. Its
mark, out of 50, is not added to the questionnaire: it lowers the pass mark instead --- full
marks bring it down to 10 out of 100.
The assignment is submitted as a pull request into the course repository: a branch, the tests
run, the pull request opened, and the submission complete once continuous integration has
passed on it.
Sample questions are published during the course, so that the format holds no surprises.
}
\end{frame}
