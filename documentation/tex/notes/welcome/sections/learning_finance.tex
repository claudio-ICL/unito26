Chapter \ref{chap.microstructure} describes order-driven markets.
Firstly,
we fold the stream of orders into an aggregated order book;
we examine several implementations of that fold,
and we compare their performance.
Secondly,
we implement Hawkes processes
as a statistical model for the arrival of orders at a trading venue,
and we study what the parametrisation of such a process says
about the statistical properties of a trading epoch.
Thirdly,
we study the order flow imbalance and price formation.

Chapter \ref{chap.options} implements pricers
for European-style call and put options,
under the Black--Scholes model and then under SABR.
We then quantify the profit and loss of trading strategies
built to capture a volatility signal.
