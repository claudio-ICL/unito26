\chapter{Limit order books}
\label{chap.lob}

This chapter opens the strand of the course devoted to market microstructure.
It describes how a modern electronic market actually works,
at the level of the individual orders that traders send to an exchange.

The material is deliberately concrete.
Before we model prices as stochastic processes,
it is worth seeing the object that produces them:
a queue of orders,
sorted by a rule,
matched by an algorithm.
Much of what later chapters assume ---
that a price exists,
that it can be observed,
that a trade can be executed at it ---
is an approximation of the mechanism described here.

The chapter can be read independently from the rest of the course.
It requires no probability beyond the notion of a counting process,
which is recalled where it is needed.
