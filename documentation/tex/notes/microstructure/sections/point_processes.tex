The order flow of an order-driven market arrives in continuous time,
and the arrival of one order changes the rate at which the next ones arrive.
This section develops the apparatus for both facts:
counting processes and their compensators,
Hawkes processes and the cluster representation,
the exponential kernel and the Markov state it buys,
and the stability and second-order theory that the price-formation section then uses.
Our main reference for the general theory is \cite[Chapter 14]{DVJ08int}.
