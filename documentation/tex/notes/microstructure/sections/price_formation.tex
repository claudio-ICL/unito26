In Sections \ref{sec.orderDrivenMarkets} and \ref{sec.messageFiles}
we described the market microstructure of order-driven markets.
In Section \ref{sec.pointProcesses}
we developed a mathematical apparatus to model
the arrival in time of orders at the trading venue.
The question that remains is the one this chapter exists to answer:
which features of the order flow reverberate into the movements of the mid-price,
and how our mathematical apparatus captures these features.

Of the statistics read off the book in Section \ref{sec.orderDrivenMarkets},
the mid-price is the one we track.
Proposition \ref{prop.lobUpdate} already says how an order moves it,
exactly and event by event,
and Section \ref{sec.messageFiles} says how to compute that from a recorded session.
Both need the whole message stream and the whole book.
We look instead for a coarser description:
a single number, read off the aggregated book, that tracks the mid-price;
a model of the flow that produces it;
and a forecast of where that flow takes the mid-price next.
